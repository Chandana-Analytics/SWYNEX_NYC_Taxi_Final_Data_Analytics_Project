# Analysis Notes



## Trip Distance



The median trip distance was 1.81 miles and the average was 3.37 miles.



About 69.1% of trips were 3 miles or less.



The 1–3 mile range contained the largest number of trips.



## Trip Duration



The median trip duration was 13 minutes and the average was 17.19 minutes.



About 88.59% of trips were completed within 30 minutes.



The 6–15 minute range contained the largest number of trips.



## Time-Based Demand



Pickup demand varied considerably throughout the day.



The lowest pickup volume occurred at 4 AM with 28,672 trips.



The highest pickup volume occurred at 6 PM with 265,569 trips.



Weekday demand also varied. Saturday had the highest average daily trip volume at 134,427 trips per day, while Sunday had the lowest at 93,409 trips per day.



## Payment Distribution



Payment types 1 and 0 together accounted for 89.61% of cleaned trips.



The analysis also compared average fare amount and average total amount across payment types.



## Pickup and Drop-off Locations



Upper East Side South had the highest pickup count with 160,343 trips.



Other high-activity pickup zones included:



- Upper East Side North

- JFK Airport

- Midtown Center

- Penn Station/Madison Sq West



The analysis also identified the most frequently used drop-off zones and pickup-to-drop-off routes.



## Long-Duration Anomalies



There were 1,432 trips with durations above 300 minutes.



Among these, 167 trips had zero recorded distance.



These records were retained because unusual values are not automatically errors. They were documented as potential anomalies for further investigation if required.



## Financial Observations



The median fare amount was $15.60 and the average was $20.80.



The median total amount was $23.05 and the average was $29.18.



The averages were higher than the medians, mainly because some higher-value trips increased the overall averages.



## Overall Observation



The analysis identified clear patterns in:



- Trip distance

- Trip duration

- Time-of-day demand

- Weekday demand

- Payment distribution

- Pickup and drop-off activity

- Unusual long-duration records



These observations were used to create the final charts and key findings for Task 2.
